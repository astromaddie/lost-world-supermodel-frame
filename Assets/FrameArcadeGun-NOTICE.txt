Prior TC2 conversion: normalized axes and grip/muzzle calibration, reconstructed
missing normals, baked base colors into a 1024-square atlas, omitted PBR maps,
and combined mesh parts. Original geometry retained: 17,108 triangles.

Lost World Frame conversion: topology, normals and scale retained; bore translated
onto OpenXR aim-space -Z so the visible barrel, pointer and shots share one ray.
The atlas is sampled into quantized per-triangle RGB materials. The original blue
plastic/metal palette is retained; the existing Red option recolors blue plastic.
Lighting is supplied by the existing gun renderer. Source and generated hashes,
muzzle calibration, and modifications are recorded in FrameArcadeGun.json.
Reproduce with Scripts/frame/import-arcade-gun.py and the approved TC2 source asset.
